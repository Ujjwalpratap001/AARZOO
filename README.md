# UDAAN 🚀

**Plan Better. Stay Focused. Move Forward.**

UDAAN is a simple and user-friendly productivity app designed to help people organise their daily activities, manage important tasks, prepare for upcoming exams, and keep track of their goals and progress — all in one place.

The app keeps getting started simple: personal or academic details are optional, so users can continue without filling in unnecessary information.

## ✨ Features

- **Dashboard:** Get a quick overview of tasks, goals, and progress.
- **Daily Tasks:** Add, view, and manage tasks in one place.
- **Upcoming Exams:** Save exam names, dates, and related details so important dates are easy to track.
- **Goals:** Set goals and monitor progress towards completing them.
- **Progress & Analytics:** View progress through visual summaries and graphs.
- **Profile:** Manage personal profile details and preferences.
- **Simple Experience:** Clear sections and easy navigation help users find what they need.
- **Optional Information:** Users can skip personal or academic details and continue using the app.

## 🎯 Purpose

UDAAN aims to make planning and self-management easier. It brings everyday tasks, important dates, goals, and progress together so users can stay organised and take small steps towards their targets.

## 📱 Getting Started

1. Install and open the app on your supported device.
2. Continue through the welcome/setup flow.
3. Add personal details only if you want to.
4. Start adding tasks, exams, and goals.
5. Check your dashboard regularly to review your progress.
